# Skill: Verify Service of Process
ID: verify-service

This skill allows the agent to cross-reference Small Claims SC-104 proof of service filings with known physical address data for L.A. Care Health Plan and its registered agents.

## Usage
- Provide a case number or filing date.
- The agent will search for related documents in the `/litigation/` or `/calevidence/` sections.
- It will verify if the address served matches the "Ghost Headquarters" (1055 W 7th St) or another verified location.
