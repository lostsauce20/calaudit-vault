TACTICAL MANUAL

Filing a Hearing Request

You may request a hearing in writing, orally, or online. For the fastest route, utilize the ACMS Portal directly at acms.dss.ca.gov (opens in a new window), or call the CDSS State Hearings Division directly at 1-800-743-8525 (TTY 1-800-952-8349).

  1. Document the Denial: Ensure you have the original Notice of Action (NOA) or documented evidence of a "Deemed Denial."
  2. ACMS Submission: Initiate the request via the CDSS portal and explicitly state if you are requesting an Expedited Hearing based on medical necessity.
  3. Verify Receipt: Always follow up via certified mail if an official acknowledgment is not received within the statutory window.

ACMS Administrator & Login Troubleshooting

  • ACMS Administrator Contact: If your ACMS user account is locked, disabled, or failing 2FA, contact the CDSS State Hearings Helpdesk at ACMS@dss.ca.gov or call 1-800-743-8525.
  • Password Resets: Use the official acms.dss.ca.gov password recovery link (opens in a new window). If you do not receive the reset email, your account may be flagged under an old county-level email address.
  • Filing Without an Account: You do not need an active ACMS administrator account to submit a time-sensitive appeal. You can submit verbally by phone or mail/fax a written request directly to the State Hearings Division.

Frequently Asked Questions

▶ What is an "Expedited" Hearing?
It is a hearing scheduled faster than the standard 90-day timeline because waiting would seriously jeopardize your life, health, or ability to attain, maintain, or regain maximum function.
▶ What is the ACMS Portal?
The Appeals Case Management System (ACMS) is the CDSS's digital portal (acms.dss.ca.gov) for filing and tracking hearing requests. It is the most direct way to initiate a state-level challenge to a health plan's decision.
▶ What evidence do I need?
You must provide the Notice of Action (NOA) from your health plan, records of grievances filed, and any clinical evidence (medical reports, letters) that support your need for the denied service.
▶ How do I log in to the CDSS ACMS Portal (acms.dss.ca.gov)?
Visit the official login page at acms.dss.ca.gov (opens in a new window). Enter your credentials or click "Register a New Account" to track existing state hearing requests, upload medical records, or file new appeals online.
▶ Who is the ACMS Administrator for CDSS state hearings?
The ACMS system administrator is operated by the California Department of Social Services State Hearings Division. For portal lockout issues, password resets, or system bugs, contact ACMS@dss.ca.gov or call 1-800-743-8525.
