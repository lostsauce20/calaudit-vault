Deadquarters Evidence · Exhibit 9

Carelon Number Traced to Massachusetts Partnership

This 58-second screen recording traces the toll-free number 833-476-1522 from a Carelon Behavioral Health contact entry associated with a California member to a Massachusetts Behavioral Health Partnership provider page displaying the same number.

What the Recording Directly Shows

The recording begins in the phone's contact and call-history interface. A contact labeled “Carelon BH” contains the toll-free number 833-476-1522. The number is copied and searched. The results include a “Mass Partnership Login Page,” which opens to a co-branded Carelon Behavioral Health and MBHP page. The same toll-free number appears on that Massachusetts-specific provider page.

Documented connection: the same telephone number appears in the California member's Carelon contact record and on a Massachusetts Behavioral Health Partnership provider page. The recording preserves the sequence used to identify that association.

Why the Trace Matters

The evidence does not merely show a generic Carelon webpage. It connects a telephone number preserved in the member's Carelon contact history to a provider portal branded for the Massachusetts Behavioral Health Partnership. For a California Medi-Cal member attempting to navigate behavioral-health administration, that cross-state association raises a concrete question about how the number was assigned, labeled, and presented in the member-facing workflow.

The page therefore preserves the exact number, the source contact label, the search path, and the destination page in one continuous recording rather than relying on a later written recollection.

Method and Evidentiary Scope

  • The recording captures the phone interface and web search continuously.
  • The number is copied directly from the “Carelon BH” contact entry rather than manually retyped from memory.
  • The query is reduced to the raw telephone number before results are reviewed.
  • The destination page is opened inside the recording, allowing the Carelon/MBHP branding and repeated number to be observed.
  • The MP4, caption file, and poster image are retained as separate evidence assets.

Important limitation

The recording documents a shared-number association and the resulting webpage trace. It does not include a live telephone call, carrier records, or network-routing data, so it does not independently prove the technical destination of every call placed to the number. The evidentiary claim is narrower: the Carelon number recorded for the California member was discoverable on a Massachusetts Behavioral Health Partnership provider page.
