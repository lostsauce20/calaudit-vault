Deadquarters Evidence · Exhibit 8

L.A. Care Address Conflict in Google Results

This 67-second screen recording preserves one continuous search sequence in which public-facing L.A. Care information displays both 1055 W. 7th Street and 1200 W. 7th Street in Los Angeles.

What the Recording Directly Shows

The user copies telephone number 213-694-1250 from a phone contact labeled “Healthcare” and searches it in Google. The search interface identifies L.A. Care Health Plan. A Google Lens result then displays 1055 W. 7th Street. After returning to the primary results and scrolling, the screen displays L.A. Care “Member Services by Plan” information with the address 1200 West 7th Street, Los Angeles, CA 90017.

Lens result 1055 W. 7th Street
Member-services information 1200 West 7th Street
Documented conflict: two different street addresses are displayed within the same investigation sequence for information associated with L.A. Care. The video preserves the query, the interfaces used, and the order in which both addresses appear.

Why the Address Conflict Matters

Address accuracy is operational, not cosmetic. Members, process servers, vendors, regulators, and other parties may rely on public-facing search and directory information when deciding where to mail records, deliver notices, seek in-person assistance, or verify an organization's location.

This exhibit does not depend on comparing screenshots captured weeks apart. It retains both addresses in a single continuous recording tied to the same telephone-number search, reducing uncertainty about whether the comparison was assembled from unrelated search sessions.

Method and Evidentiary Scope

  • The investigation begins with a telephone number copied directly from the phone contact record.
  • The number is pasted into Google rather than reconstructed from memory.
  • The recording captures the Google identification, Lens result, return to the main results, and member-services address continuously.
  • Both address strings are visible in the retained MP4 and described in the WebVTT caption file.
  • The MP4, caption file, and poster image are maintained as separate evidence assets.

Important limitation

The recording establishes that Google interfaces displayed two different addresses associated with L.A. Care information during the captured search. It does not, by itself, establish which address was legally current, whether one address served a different organizational function, or which underlying source caused each result. Those questions require comparison with dated official records. The preserved claim is the public-facing inconsistency itself.
