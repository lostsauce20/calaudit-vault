CLINICAL ROOM FAQ

Common Questions on Clinical Screenings, Withdrawal Protocols, and Privacy

What is the difference between the CIWA-Ar and CIWA-B scales?

The CIWA-Ar (Clinical Institute Withdrawal Assessment for Alcohol) is specifically designed and validated for assessing alcohol withdrawal severity. The CIWA-B (Clinical Institute Withdrawal Assessment for Benzodiazepines) is adapted specifically to measure the unique withdrawal profile of benzodiazepines, which has different symptom durations, rebound anxiety profiles, and cognitive manifestations.

How is the COWS scale utilized in clinical settings?

The COWS (Clinical Opiate Withdrawal Scale) is an 11-item clinical tool used to determine the severity of opioid withdrawal. It is commonly used by Substance Use Navigators (SUNs) and medical providers to time the safe initiation of buprenorphine (Suboxone) or methadone treatment, helping prevent precipitated withdrawal.

Are these screening worksheets considered official diagnostic tools?

No. These screening tools (such as the PHQ-9 for depression, GAD-7 for anxiety, and ASRS-v1.1 for ADHD) are highly validated diagnostic aids used to measure symptom severity and establish clinical baselines. However, they are screening tools; a formal clinical diagnosis requires evaluation by a licensed healthcare practitioner, therapist, or psychiatrist.

What is NEXT Distro and what is their role in harm reduction?

NEXT Distro is a national, online-first harm reduction organization that provides sterile supplies, naloxone, and evidence-based educational materials directly to individuals. Their withdrawal management and safer-use guides hosted here are certified, peer-vetted clinical resources for individuals seeking to reduce risks or safely manage tapering at home.

How does the CA Bridge model change hospital-based substance use treatment?

The CA Bridge model advocates for immediate, low-barrier access to medication-assisted treatment (MAT) in emergency departments and hospital wards. By placing Substance Use Navigators (SUNs) in hospitals and initiating buprenorphine or methadone quickly, the model bridges the gap between acute crisis care and long-term outpatient recovery.

Will downloading or viewing these clinical scales compromise my privacy?

No. Every clinical checklist and protocol worksheet hosted here is a static PDF file served directly to your browser sandbox. No input, scoring data, or download metrics are ever collected, processed, or uploaded, ensuring absolute clinical and forensic privacy.
