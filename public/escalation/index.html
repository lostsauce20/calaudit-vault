Strategic Advocacy Protocols

CalAudit’s escalation sequence preserves the record at the plan level, protects time-sensitive benefits, and then routes the dispute to the regulator with jurisdiction. Use the linked CalAudit guides for the complete filing requirements.

  • Plan-Level Grievance Exhaustion: Commercial health disputes under DMHC jurisdiction require filing a formal grievance with the plan prior to state intervention, except where expedited criteria apply. Urgent requests route to the DMHC Help Center immediately. See CalAudit’s Knox-Keene appeal rights guide and DMHC complaint guide.
  • Aid Paid Pending Preservation: For DHCS public Medi-Cal service reductions or adjustments, file for a CDSS State Hearing prior to the effective enforcement date and explicitly state requirements for Aid Paid Pending to maintain current care coverage. Follow CalAudit’s Medi-Cal State Hearing guide.
  • Anonymity Restraints: Regulatory bodies accept anonymous filings, but cannot subpoena specific clinical records without an explicit, signed patient waiver format.
  • Dual-Channel Fraud Reporting: Systemic compliance or benefits fraud within federally subsidized Medi-Cal structures must be filed concurrently with the California DOJ DMFEA (opens in a new window) and federal HHS OIG (opens in a new window) systems to align jurisdictional tracking. Use CalAudit’s HHS OIG filing guide to prepare the federal submission.
