CalAudit Evidence Room · Exhibit 18

JWCH System-Wide Carrier Intercept & Phone Collapse

Recorded June 18, 2026. This exhibit documents three separate John Wesley Health Centers phone numbers returning Verizon carrier intercept recordings while an out-of-network control call placed from the same handset connects successfully.

What this evidence shows

The audit tests three numbers associated with JWCH: the central toll-free scheduling line, a regional main line, and the Palmdale clinic line. Each JWCH call is rejected at the carrier-intercept level. The caller then places a control call to an unrelated out-of-network number using the same phone and carrier connection. That call completes, demonstrating that the handset was capable of placing an outbound call during the recording.

The control call does not independently establish why the JWCH numbers failed. It documents the observable result: all three tested JWCH numbers were inaccessible during the recorded sequence while the unrelated comparison number connected.

Methodology and limits

  • All calls were placed in one recorded sequence from the same handset.
  • The comparison call was placed after the three JWCH failures.
  • The recording documents call outcomes and carrier announcements; it does not identify the internal technical cause of the JWCH failures.
  • The video length is 1 minute 12 seconds.

Why this evidence matters

Healthcare providers receiving federal financial assistance have obligations under Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act and Section 1557 of the Affordable Care Act to provide meaningful and equal access to their programs and services. Telephone access points used for scheduling, patient communication, or service navigation are part of the practical pathway by which patients access care. This exhibit documents a recorded instance in which multiple JWCH-associated telephone numbers returned carrier intercept failures while a control call successfully connected.

The significance of this recording is the documented loss of telephone accessibility at the point of contact. The video preserves the exact numbers tested, the carrier announcements returned, and the successful comparison call, creating a time-stamped record of the accessibility condition observed during the test.

The recording does not establish the technical reason the JWCH numbers failed. It documents the observable outcome: JWCH-associated contact numbers returned carrier intercept messages instead of connecting during the recorded sequence.
