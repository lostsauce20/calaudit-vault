Frequently Asked Questions

▶ What is a "Forensic Auditor"?
In this context, it refers to the application of investigative data analysis, systems auditing, and statutory research to institutions that rely on administrative complexity to evade accountability.
▶ Is this legal advice?
No. This is a proprietary forensic repository documenting physical audits. All steps are compiled from publicly available California statutes, federal regulations, and concrete administrative tracking.
▶ Why focus on L.A. Care?
As California's largest public health plan, operating with substantial public funding to administer Medi-Cal to millions of residents, L.A. Care's operational compliance is a matter of direct public interest. Bound by statutory mandates under the Knox-Keene Act, the plan has a legal obligation to maintain accessible provider networks, resolve member grievances, and adjudicate claims on time. This audit documents verified operational barriers—specifically provider directory inaccuracies (ghost networks), administrative processing delays, and statutory defaults—to establish empirical accountability and protect beneficiary access to covered care.
