STATE HEARING DELAYS AND APPEAL TRACKING

Documenting timelines, expedited-review denials, and unresolved Medi-Cal appeal issues

THE STORY AT A GLANCE

When a California state agency reviews a disputed Medi-Cal decision, several statutory and administrative timelines can apply. This page documents my experience with the California Department of Social Services (CDSS), including expedited-review denials, ACMS portal activity, and delays affecting the underlying hearing and reimbursement disputes.

Statutory Timeline Dashboard

Comparing stated legal timelines with the timeline documented in this case.

AB 3275 TIMELINE

30 Days Max

State law establishes a 30-day decision, payment, or denial timeline for certain Medi-Cal matters.

APL 07-002 TIMELINE

120 Days

A legacy DHCS policy from 2007 provides a 120-day review period for certain Conlan reimbursement claims.

The Paper Trail Breakdown

Step-by-step documentation of the delays from March to June 2026

  1. March 15, 2026

    Urgent Hearing Request Filed

    I submitted an urgent request for an expedited State Fair Hearing concerning the unresolved medical-access dispute.

  2. March 18 – 19, 2026

    Acknowledgment and Postmark Timing

    The acknowledgment paperwork is dated March 18, while the envelope is postmarked March 19. This was also the only related document that did not appear in the ACMS portal, so both dates are preserved here as part of the record.

  3. April 13 – 14, 2026

    Medical Records Submitted; Expedite Denied

    I uploaded provider records documenting a high risk of seizure or other harm. Less than 24 hours later, the portal issued a denial of the request to expedite the hearing.

  4. April 20, 2026

    Medical Necessity Statement Submitted

    My out of pocket NP (a true health care professional, not an insurance employee) steps up, submitting a formal statement certifying absolute, immediate medical necessity.

  5. May 7, 2026

    Dismissal Request Filed

    The state's billing division filed a request to dismiss the reimbursement-related appeal on the ground that the underlying claim was still under review.

  6. May 11 – 15, 2026

    Second Expedite Denial and Proposed Dismissal

    A second expedited request was denied. The state then issued a proposed dismissal notice citing the 120-day review period under the older Conlan reimbursement policy.

  7. May 27, 2026

    Formal Opposition Filed

    I uploaded a formal legal opposition challenging the proposed dismissal and citing AB 3275 and the applicable Medi-Cal timelines.

  8. June 2 – 3, 2026

    Gainwell Follow-Up Call

    After I uploaded screenshots showing that my out-of-pocket care program had been cancelled after my personal funds were exhausted while the $2,307 reimbursement remained unresolved, Gainwell Technologies—the DHCS contractor handling Conlan claims—called me the next day. During that call, they requested March superbills for both providers. The timing shows that the new documentation was reviewed promptly and led to additional follow-up on the reimbursement claim.

  9. June 15, 2026

    90-Day Hearing Timeline Passed

    The original State Fair Hearing request remained unscheduled beyond the 90-day timeline cited in 42 CFR § 431.244 and MPP § 22-060.1.

  10. June 16, 2026

    Notice of Default Filed

    I filed a formal Notice of Statutory Exhaustion and CDSS Default in the portal, documenting that the cited hearing deadline had passed and preserving my position regarding administrative exhaustion.

  11. June 22, 2026

    Final Dismissal of Conlan Reimbursement Appeal

    The State Hearings Division issued a **Final Dismissal (Non-Hearable Issue)** for the Conlan reimbursement dispute. The notice states that the Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) Conlan Unit has **120 days** to review the claim and that the reimbursement dispute was not yet hearable through CDSS. The letter did not identify a separate expedited hearing procedure for that reimbursement issue.
