[ FORENSIC FAQ ]

Are my images safe here?
The selected image is processed by this page with the Canvas API. Review your browser, extensions, and device environment before using sensitive files.
Why use PNG over JPEG for the output?
PNG avoids the additional lossy compression introduced by JPEG, so it is the preferred output for retaining the generated effect.
Will humans notice the change?
The effect appears as grain or texture. Visibility varies with the image and intensity setting, and the effect is not a biometric-protection guarantee.
