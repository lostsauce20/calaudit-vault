[ SECURITY PROTOCOLS & SPECIFICATIONS ]

LOCAL, IMAGE-BASED PROCESSING

The page renders the selected PDF into canvases and paints redaction boxes into those page images before export. The resulting PDF no longer contains the source text layer, links, forms, annotations, or accessibility structure.

VERIFY BEFORE DISCLOSURE

This utility is not a compliance certification or a substitute for an approved redaction workflow. Review every exported page and confirm that the tool fits your organization's technical, legal, and records-retention requirements.

[ STEP-BY-STEP PRIVACY MANUAL ]

  1. Stage 1 // Load Document: Drag a PDF into the dashed zone above, or click to browse and select a file.
  2. Stage 2 // Local Rendering: Each page is rendered onto an in-browser canvas. No page data is transmitted anywhere.
  3. Stage 3 // Draw Redaction Nodes: Click and drag over any page to mark a redaction box. Repeat across as many regions and pages as needed.
  4. Stage 4 // Undo if Needed: Press Ctrl+Z (or Cmd+Z) to remove the most recently drawn redaction node.
  5. Stage 5 // Compile & Export: Click "Compile & Export PDF" to hard-bake every redaction into the page image and download a sanitized copy.

[ WHY FLATTENED REDACTION NEEDS VERIFICATION ]

A visual black rectangle is not always a redaction: some PDF editors leave the original text layer intact beneath an annotation. This utility instead exports image-based pages with the selected regions painted into the pixels, but that conversion also removes searchability, links, form fields, and document accessibility.

The CalAudit PDF Redaction Tool performs its document manipulation in the current browser page and exports a new PDF. Inspect the complete output at high zoom, test text selection, and follow any required organizational review process before sharing a sensitive record.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ]

▶ Is my document uploaded anywhere?
No. The PDF is parsed and rendered entirely in your browser using pdf.js. Nothing is sent to a server at any point in the process, from upload through export.
▶ Is the redacted text actually removed, or just covered up?
Actually removed. Each page is flattened to an image, and redaction boxes are drawn as solid black pixels directly into that image before export. There's no underlying text layer left to copy or extract from the final PDF.
▶ Can I undo a redaction box by mistake?
Yes. Press Ctrl+Z (or Cmd+Z on Mac) at any time to remove the most recently drawn redaction box. This works across all pages in the current session.
▶ Does this tool work offline?
Yes. Once the page and its libraries have loaded, you can disconnect from the internet entirely and continue redacting and exporting documents with full offline autonomy.
▶ Is this suitable for HIPAA-sensitive medical records?
The tool is not a HIPAA certification or legal-compliance guarantee. Confirm that local browser processing, image-based export, output review, device controls, and records handling fit your organization's approved workflow before using it with protected health information.
